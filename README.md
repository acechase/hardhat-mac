# Hardhat Mac

A phone-friendly construction-site platformer in the spirit of the 1983 classic. Three sites: bolt the steel frame, collect the lunchboxes, and feed the crates into the hopper.

**Play:** https://acechase.github.io/hardhat-mac/

On iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen** to play full screen with no browser bars.

Controls: the pad walks and climbs ladders, JUMP jumps (tapping the playfield also jumps). On a keyboard, use the arrow keys and space.

The whole game is one file, `index.html`, with no build step.
